How the numbers are made

    
Sunspots are detected in the Mastcam-Z image alone. SDO/HMI is used afterwards, only as the reference.

    
A Mastcam-Z sunspot covers a few pixels, so its shape is not resolved. What the camera measures is the light
      the spot removes.

    
The HMI image provides each group’s total area and umbral area, and its light deficit converted to the
      Mastcam-Z wavelength (HMI Adef). Adef compared with HMI Adef is the most
      direct comparison, because both measure the same quantity in the same band.

    
Positions are heliographic latitude and Stonyhurst longitude (0° points towards Earth) at the Mastcam-Z time.
      A detection must reach the significance threshold given in each summary. The threshold was set on the near
      side so that false detections are rare.
